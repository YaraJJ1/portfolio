    What I work on

    Mostly the systems underneath a game: how things move, collide and look. A full list of tools is on the skills page.

    • Gameplay & physics

      Movement and vehicle controllers, grappling hooks, and buoyancy that lets objects ride on moving water.

    • Graphics & shaders

      Real-time GPU work in URP and HLSL, like an FFT ocean simulated in compute shaders.

    • Animation & tools

      Procedural animation for multi-legged characters, plus desktop and web tools in JavaScript.
